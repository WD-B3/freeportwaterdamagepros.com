Frost, curb stops and who owns which pipe

The Village of Lena Water Department keeps the service line between the main and the curb stop in good repair. The owner installs and pays for everything from the curb stop to the building, and must keep service pipes and fixtures in good repair and protected from frost at the owner's own expense.

The village code also says it does not pay claims for interruptions or damage caused by broken pipes. In practice, a pipe that splits inside your wall is yours to deal with, and a frozen pipe is where the cleanup begins. Our frozen pipe water damage page explains what to do at thaw.

Village Hall is at 122 E. Main Street, phone (815) 369-4016, open Monday through Friday from 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Ask there about your shut-off before winter, not during a break.

When water is spreading in Lena

In a northern Illinois village, a burst or frozen line in the cold months is a common emergency, and basement water after a long rain is another. Both are handled the same way: extract, find the wet framing, dry it and document the loss.

If your emergency is a split supply line, see burst pipe water damage. For water in a cellar, see basement water damage cleanup. Water that reaches a ceiling from a roof or upstairs bath is handled on ceiling water damage repair.

If water is still spreading, take these steps

Close the valve inside

The main valve in the building is yours. If it will not turn, ask Village Hall about the curb stop outside.

Dry panel, then power

Turn off power to wet rooms only if you can reach a dry panel. If not, stay out and phone the utility.

Raise the contents

Lift anything on the floor onto tables or stairs. Pull up rugs you do not want soaked.

Keep records

Photograph the break and the damage. The village does not pay pipe damage claims, so your insurer needs the record.

Insurance and records when the village will not cover pipe damage

Because the village does not pay claims for broken pipes, your homeowners or renters policy is the main source of help. The Illinois Department of Insurance says sudden damage, such as a burst pipe, may be covered, while ongoing leaks and maintenance problems are typically excluded. Report the loss quickly and stop the source of the water.

The department also advises making only reasonable repairs until the insurer has inspected the damage. Keep the broken section of pipe, take wide and close photos and save receipts for emergency steps. Our written scope lists affected rooms, materials and drying equipment, which gives your adjuster a clear record.

Work in Lena follows the same ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage restoration standard we use everywhere, including water categories, drying goals and documented moisture readings.
