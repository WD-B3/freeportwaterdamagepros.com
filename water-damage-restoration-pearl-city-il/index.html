What the rain years mean for homes here

In 2022, 7 to 12 inches of rain fell over 48 hours in parts of Stephenson County, and Illinois Route 73 between Pearl City and Lena was closed. Roads that close cut off access for any crew, so if you live north of downtown and water is rising, call early instead of waiting for a crest.

Cleanup after floodwater follows the steps on our flood damage cleanup page. Floodwater is Category 3, so wet carpet, pad and low drywall are usually removed instead of dried in place.

When water is spreading in Pearl City

Village profile

A trustee-village of 790 residents in Loran Township, about 12 miles west of Freeport and in ZIP 61062.

Around the clock

Emergency dispatch is available around the clock, every day. After a regional storm, we handle calls as roads reopen.

Water and sewer

The village bills water and sewer as a flat rate coupon, so there is no meter to show a leak on your bill.

Shut-offs

Scheduled shut-offs go through Village Hall at (815) 443-2141, and emergencies or after hours at (815) 541-0464.

The flat-rate billing matters. In a metered town, a jump in the water bill can reveal a hidden leak. Here, you learn about leaks from the floor, the smell and the sound. If you hear water running with every fixture off, close the main and call.

Storm water and basement trouble

After heavy rain, the usual problems are basement seepage, sump pump failure and rain through a roof or wall. Our storm water damage cleanup page covers openings in the envelope, and our basement water damage cleanup page covers below-grade rooms.

Churches, American Legion Post 1014 and a small business district anchor the village. Commercial and community buildings follow the same steps, and our commercial water damage page shows how zoned drying can keep part of a building usable.

Before we arrive, if water is still spreading

Go up and out

If water is rising, move people and pets to higher ground first. Do not drive through flooded roads.

Stop what you can

Shut off the main valve if a pipe is the source. Call the village after-hours number, (815) 541-0464, for the curb valve.

Power safety

Leave breakers alone if the panel or floor is wet. Phone the electric utility from a dry spot.

Save the record

Photograph the water line on walls and belongings. Keep damaged materials for the adjuster.

Nearby towns

Lena lies north along Route 73 and is covered on our Lena page. For crews heading to the southeast, see German Valley.

Flood coverage for homes north of downtown

The Illinois Department of Insurance states that homeowners policies exclude flood damage and that flood insurance typically takes effect 30 days after purchase. For a village that has flooded more than once, that waiting period is the key detail. A policy bought after the water arrives will not help with that loss.

Check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your address, then ask your agent about a flood policy and a sewer backup endorsement. Keep photos of the water line on your walls and any damaged belongings. If you rent, tell your landlord in writing and ask whether the building carries its own flood coverage.

Roads, access and when to call

Crews cannot work in a flooded roadway, so timing matters. When a storm is forecast, a call before the creek or river crests lets dispatch plan around likely road closures. After the water drops, drying should start quickly, using the steps on our structural drying page.
