Water heater leak cleanup in Freeport, IL begins when a tank, a connector or a relief valve lets go and the utility area fills with hot or warm water. If you hear hissing or see a puddle, call (815) 314-9281 at any hour. Turn off the power or gas to the heater, close the cold-water inlet and keep clear of any scalding water.

A tank is a stored volume of water. A typical residential tank holds 40 to 50 gallons, and a full failure can put all of it on the floor in minutes. A slow weep at a fitting is smaller, but it can run for weeks inside a closet or on a finished basement floor.

Water heater leak emergency: steps before the crew arrives

Cut the energy

For electric heaters, switch off the breaker if you can reach the panel dry. For gas, turn the gas valve on the heater to off. If you smell gas, leave and call the gas utility or 911.

Close the cold inlet

The shut-off valve on the cold water pipe above the tank stops refilling. If it fails, use the main valve.

Mind the heat

Water from a failing tank can be hot enough to burn. Wait for it to cool before stepping in it.

Lift what is on the floor

Boxes, laundry and storage left on the basement floor will wick water up. Move them to shelves.

Why heater leaks are often hidden

A tank can fail slowly. Corrosion eats through the steel from the inside, and the first sign is a damp patch under the unit, a rust stain or a sound like dripping. Because most heaters sit in a utility room, closet or corner of a basement, small leaks can run unseen behind a wall or under flooring.

A sudden spike in your water bill can also point to a leak. If you notice an unusual jump, check the heater, the pipes near it and any floor drain. The City of Freeport billing office at 815-233-0111 can answer questions about your usage.

Cleanup, step by step

We extract the water with pumps and wet vacuums, then check the surrounding walls and floors with moisture meters. Wet carpet and pad are removed. Drywall near the floor is inspected and cut back when it has wicked water above the baseboard.

Air movers and dehumidifiers run until readings fall to dry standards, which is covered on our structural drying and dehumidification page. Plumbing and heater replacement belong to a licensed plumber, and we schedule drying around that work.

When a water heater leaks

Turn off the power or gas to the heater and close the cold water supply valve. Do not touch a unit that is sparking, smells of gas or is surrounded by standing water. Hot water can scald, so let a leaking tank cool before handling any fittings.

Claims and documentation

The Illinois Department of Insurance notes that sudden water damage, such as a burst pipe, may be covered, while ongoing leaks and maintenance problems are typically excluded. A heater that fails without warning is often treated as sudden, while one that has leaked for months may not be.

Photograph the heater, the label with its age and the wet area. Keep the old unit if the plumber removes it, since an insurer may ask to see it.

Should I turn off the heater if it is leaking?

Yes, cut the power or gas first, then close the cold water inlet. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911.

Can I replace the water heater myself?

Replacement involves gas, electrical and water connections, so most people hire a licensed plumber. We handle the water damage left behind.

Is water heater damage covered by insurance?

Sudden failures may be covered, but slow leaks and wear are often not. Read your policy and ask your insurer.
