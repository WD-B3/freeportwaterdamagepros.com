Drain backing up with dark water? That is a sewage backup

Callers often describe this in plain terms. The basement drain is gurgling, the floor drain is spilling dark water or the toilet will not flush and a foul smell fills the lower level. The trade name for what follows is sewage backup cleanup, and it follows rules for contaminated water.

Sewage emergency: what to do first

Stay out and avoid contact

Do not touch the water with bare skin and do not walk through it. Do not run a regular vacuum on it, and do not try to clean it with towels. If electrical outlets or equipment are in the water, stay out and call the electric utility or 911 before calling us.

Stop adding water

Do not flush, run the washer or use sinks. Every fixture on the line adds to the problem.

Find out whose pipe it is

If the backup reaches many homes, the city main may be involved. The City of Freeport Water and Sewer Division can be reached at 815-233-0111 and after hours at 815-233-0711.

Call a plumber for the line

A clog in your private line needs a plumber to clear it. We handle cleanup of what came out.

Photograph the area

Take pictures before cleanup for your insurer.

Why sewage water is Category 3

ANSI/IICRC S500 treats sewage as Category 3, the most contaminated water category. It carries bacteria and other pathogens, so porous materials that touched it are usually removed and replaced, not cleaned. That includes carpet, pad, drywall and insulation within reach of the water.

Freeport's sewer history and what it means for you

An EPA case study on Freeport notes that the city has separate sanitary and storm sewers and a history of wet-weather sewer problems tied to its varied terrain. In 2001, a hundred-year rain event flooded the Homer Street area because storm sewer capacity was inadequate, and the city later bought and razed homes along a covered creek there.

The practical point is that heavy rain can load sewers and cause backups in low basements. If it happens to you, ask the Water and Sewer Division whether neighbors have reported the same thing.

Cleanup, safely

Crews wear protective gear. Liquids are extracted with pumps and wet vacuums. Porous materials that touched sewage are removed and bagged. Hard surfaces are cleaned and treated with an antimicrobial, then the area is dried.

The drying phase is the same equipment and logging covered on our structural drying page. For basements, see basement water damage cleanup. If the backup came with outside flooding, see flood damage cleanup.

Insurance: sewer backup is usually a separate endorsement

The Illinois Department of Insurance notes that sewer backup or sump pump failure may be covered only if you have that endorsement. Check your policy for the words sewer backup or water backup. Report the loss quickly, photograph the damage and keep damaged materials until the adjuster has looked at them.

Do you handle sewage backup emergencies at night or on weekends in Freeport?

Can I clean up a small sewage backup myself?

It is not advised. Sewage carries pathogens, and porous materials hold contamination. Professionals use protective gear and remove what cannot be cleaned.

Who fixes the clogged line?

A plumber. If many homes are affected, the city main may be involved, so call the Water and Sewer Division.

Does insurance cover a sewer backup?

Often only with a sewer backup endorsement. Check your policy.
