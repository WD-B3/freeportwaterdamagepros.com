Water Damage and Electronics: When to Unplug in Freeport, IL

24/7 Emergency Line(815) 314-9281

By the Freeport Water Damage Pros team · Updated November 9, 2026

Water and electricity are the dangerous pair in any leak. The first question is not whether a device can be saved but whether anyone can touch it safely. Once everyone is out of harm's way, there are sensible steps that give wet electronics a fair chance.

Safety first: power and water

If water is near outlets or the panel

Do not step into standing water that may reach outlets, power strips or appliances. If the electrical panel is dry and you can reach it without touching water, switch off the breakers for the wet area. If you cannot reach it safely, leave and call the electric utility or 911.

Never use a wet hand or stand in water to unplug something. A device that is still plugged into a live outlet in a flooded room can energize the water itself. The safe move is to shut off power at the panel from dry ground, and only then to handle cords.

What happens to wet electronics

Water short-circuits live circuits and, over days, corrodes metal contacts. Clean water does less harm than dirty water, and a device that was off when it got wet has a better outlook than one that was running. Salt, sewage and floodwater carry minerals and contaminants that corrode quickly.

Speed matters because corrosion keeps going after the water is gone. Taking a device to a technician soon after it gets wet often gives a better result than letting it sit for a week.

Outlets, wiring and the electrical panel

Wet outlets, switch boxes and wiring need a licensed electrician before they are used again. Wiring inside wet walls can stay damp long after the surface dries, so drying and an electrical inspection often happen together. Our crews work with moisture meters and thermal cameras to find these wet areas.

If a ceiling light is full of water, leave its switch alone and shut off that circuit from the panel only if it is dry and reachable. Our ceiling water damage repair page explains how water behaves above a ceiling.

A fast plan for a flood warning

  • Unplug TVs, computers and chargers from the wall
  • Move electronics to a high shelf or upper floor
  • Photograph serial numbers before moving them
  • Switch off the breaker for the basement if it is dry and reachable
  • Bring phones and laptops with you

These steps take minutes and can save thousands of dollars of equipment. They only work if you know your plan before the water arrives.

What technicians can and cannot recover

A repair shop can sometimes recover data from a wet drive or phone if it is dried and opened promptly. They cannot promise results. Ask about their process, whether they charge for evaluation and what happens if recovery fails. Keep backups of important files in more than one place, and treat the original device as a bonus.

For a wet ceiling above a home office, see ceiling water damage repair before you turn anything back on.

Nearby villages and power outages

In Lena, Orangeville and the rest of the county, a storm that floods a basement can also knock out power. When the lights come back on, equipment that was left plugged in can surge into a wet room. Unplug first, then check the area before restoring power.

Keep a flashlight and a battery radio near the panel so you can work safely in the dark.

Freeport flood alerts and the lift-and-unplug plan

The City of Freeport runs an emergency alert text service. Texting FREEPORTALERTS to 91896 signs you up, according to the city website. When a river is rising, an alert gives you time to unplug and lift electronics before water arrives.

For homes close to the Pecatonica, where the gauge lists a flood stage of 13.0 feet, a plan to move electronics upstairs is more useful than a plan to repair them afterward.

Electronics in a flood or sewage backup

Floodwater and sewage damage electronics beyond practical repair in most cases, and they bring health hazards. Treat anything that was submerged as likely lost, but photograph it for the insurer and ask a technician before throwing out high-value items. Details on the water categories are in flood damage cleanup.

If you live in Pearl City or another village near a creek, include power-down steps in your emergency plan, since roads can close.

Insurance for electronics

Homeowners and renters policies often cover personal property after a covered event, but flood is excluded from standard policies according to the Illinois Department of Insurance. Keep model numbers, photos and receipts. A home inventory app can speed the process.

Common questions

Should I put a wet phone in rice?

It does not remove water well. Power it off and take it to a technician instead.

Can I turn on a TV after a leak dried?

Not until a technician checks it. Internal moisture can cause a short circuit.

How do I sign up for Freeport emergency alerts?

Text FREEPORTALERTS to 91896, per the City of Freeport website.

Is a wet outlet always dangerous?

It can be. Treat wet outlets as unsafe until an electrician has checked them.
