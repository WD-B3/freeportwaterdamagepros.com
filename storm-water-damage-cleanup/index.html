Storm emergency: what to do now

Stay safe first

Do not go outside during lightning or high wind. Keep away from downed lines and from any room where ceilings sag.

Limit the water

Move buckets under drips and pull furniture away from windows. Place towels along sills.

Cover openings if you can

A tarp on a broken window or hole in the roof limits more rain. Do not climb on a wet roof yourself.

Photograph everything

Document damage inside and out for your insurance claim.

What Freeport rain can do

In 2022, news reports described 7 to 12 inches of rain in 48 hours across parts of Stephenson County. Illinois Route 73 between Pearl City and Lena was closed, as were Business Highway 20 west of Freeport and Route 75 east of the city. The Pecatonica reached 15.4 feet against a flood stage of 13.0.

A storm like that tests everything at once. Roofs shed water, basements take on seepage and storm drains fill. After a heavy rain, check the attic, windows and the basement floor even if the house looks fine from the outside.

How we clean up after a storm

We find where the water entered and where it went. Moisture meters and thermal imaging show wet insulation and wall cavities. Standing water is extracted. Wet materials that cannot dry in place are removed, and drying equipment is placed.

Roof and exterior repair belong to roofers and contractors. We work around their schedule so tarping is not delayed. For the drying process, see structural drying.

Storm water is usually treated as contaminated under the ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage restoration standard, which is why we separate it from a clean pipe leak when we plan the work.

Storm claims and repair scams

Sudden storm damage is often covered by a homeowners policy, while flood damage usually is not. The Illinois Department of Insurance advises reporting the loss quickly and not making large permanent repairs until the insurer has inspected. Keep temporary repair receipts.

The Illinois Attorney General warns about contractors who pressure homeowners after a storm. A written contract with all costs is required for home repair over $1,000, and you have three business days to cancel if the sale is made and signed at your home.

Storm Water Damage questions in Freeport

Do you handle storm water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Freeport?

Yes. The line is open 24/7, including during active weather. Same-day response is offered when available, and no arrival time is guaranteed.

Will you tarp my roof?

Roofing contractors handle tarping and repairs. We handle water removal and drying inside the home.

Is storm water damage covered by homeowners insurance?

Wind and rain damage that enters through a storm-damaged opening is often covered, but flood is usually not. Ask your insurer.

How soon should I dry out after a storm?

As soon as safely possible. Wet materials left in place can develop problems within days.
