Water Damage Restoration Services in Freeport, IL

Nineteen services grouped by how the water got in, all answered by one 24/7 emergency line.

24/7 Emergency Line(815) 314-9281

Every service below starts the same way. We stop the damage, find where the water traveled and dry the structure with documented readings. What changes is the cause, the water category and the materials involved. Pick the page that matches your problem, or call the 24/7 emergency line and we will point you to the right one.

We cover water damage restoration only. We do not list mold remediation or fire damage services.

What to do before you call

  • Shut off the water at the source if it is safe to reach
  • Do not touch outlets, cords or appliances near standing water
  • Take photos and video of the damage for your insurer
  • Call 911 first for any electrical, gas or structural danger
