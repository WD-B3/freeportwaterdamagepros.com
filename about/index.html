About Freeport Water Damage Pros

A local water damage restoration service with a 24/7 emergency line and written scopes.

24/7 Emergency Line(815) 314-9281

Freeport Water Damage Pros is a water damage restoration service for homes and businesses in Freeport, Illinois and the villages around it. We answer an emergency line 24/7, we explain the scope and the price before work starts, and we keep records you can use with your insurer.

How we work

Every job follows the same order. Stop or confirm the source, extract standing water, find hidden moisture with meters and thermal cameras, remove materials that cannot be saved, dry the structure and document it. We work to the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration.

We explain the water category before we touch a surface, because clean water, gray water and sewage call for different decisions. You will see the plan before work starts, and you can compare it with any other company's plan.

What we do not do

We do not remove mold and we do not handle fire damage. If you find visible mold, ask a mold contractor about the ANSI/IICRC S520 standard and Illinois licensing. We also do not decide insurance claims or promise outcomes. Your insurer does that.

Where we work

Our address is 1601 S West Ave, Freeport, IL 61032. We serve Freeport, Lena, Orangeville, Pearl City and German Valley. Our service area page explains how coverage works and what to have ready when you call.

Why local knowledge matters

The Pecatonica River, Yellow Creek and a sewer system with a history of wet-weather problems shape the kinds of losses this area sees. Our service pages and town pages are written around those facts, with sources, so you can judge them yourself.

Contact

Call (815) 314-9281 any hour. You can also write to contact@freeportwaterdamagepros.com, though the phone is faster in an emergency. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.
