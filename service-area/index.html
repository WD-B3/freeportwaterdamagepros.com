Other towns we take calls from

Cedarville (61013) and Dakota (61018) are covered on the Orangeville page. We also hear from Eleroy (61027), Baileyville (61007) in Ogle County, and Rockford. We do not publish distance or arrival promises, so call with your address and we will tell you plainly whether we can serve it.

What to have ready when you call

  • Your address and the nearest cross street
  • Where the water came from and whether it is stopped
  • Whether it is clean supply water, appliance water or sewage
  • Which rooms and floors are wet
  • Your insurer name if you have already called
